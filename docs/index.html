mic-monitor

Hear your own microphone in your headphones, with low delay.

For anyone recording, streaming or calling with a USB mic and closed headphones who wants to hear themselves. It skips the lag of Windows' "Listen to this device", and it keeps working when a wireless headset sleeps and wakes up. Free and open source.

Install

Windows

Open PowerShell and paste:

irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/robworks-code/mic-monitor/main/install.ps1 | iex

macOS or Linux

Open Terminal and paste:

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/robworks-code/mic-monitor/main/install.sh | sh

Installs Python if you need it, keeps everything in your user folder, and starts the tray icon. Run the same line again to update. Nothing starts at login until you tick Start at login in the tray menu.

Use it

  • Tray icon: click to turn monitoring on or off. Green means on, grey means off. Right-click to pick devices, volume and latency, or to start at login.
  • Terminal: type mic-monitor to toggle.
  • Pick devices by part of their name, once: mic-monitor config --in Yeti --out Headset

Commands

mic-monitortoggle on or off
mic-monitor statuswhat is running, and which devices
mic-monitor listshow your audio devices
mic-monitor configshow or save devices and volume
mic-monitor-traystart the tray icon

Why the delay is low

  • Streams the mic straight to your headphones with a small buffer.
  • On Windows it always uses the low-latency WASAPI path instead of the slower MME one.
  • A wireless headset adds its own delay; a wired path or the mic's own headphone jack is faster. See the README's Latency section.
  • When a wireless headset wakes up, the sound comes back by itself.
  • On Windows, change your default mic in Sound settings and monitoring moves to it.

Uninstall

Windows

& ([scriptblock]::Create((irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/robworks-code/mic-monitor/main/install.ps1))) -Uninstall

macOS or Linux

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/robworks-code/mic-monitor/main/install.sh | sh -s -- --uninstall

Your saved settings and logs are kept.
